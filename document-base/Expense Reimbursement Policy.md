# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy Owner:** Finance  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All U.S. employees

## 1. Purpose

The company reimburses employees for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred while conducting company business.

## 2. General Requirements

Expenses must:

- Have a legitimate business purpose.
- Be reasonable in amount.
- Be incurred while performing authorized company business.
- Be submitted with sufficient supporting documentation.

Employees must not submit personal expenses as business expenses.

## 3. Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for individual expenses of **$50 or more**.

Credit-card statements alone are not considered sufficient documentation when a receipt is reasonably available.

## 4. Submission Deadline

Employees must submit expenses within **15 calendar days** after the expense is incurred or, for business travel, within 15 calendar days after returning from the trip.

Late submissions may require additional manager approval.

## 5. Business Meals

Business meals with customers, partners, or prospective customers must include:

- Names of attendees.
- Company affiliation.
- Business purpose.
- Date and location.

Alcohol may be reimbursed only when consumed as part of an otherwise legitimate business meal and when reasonable under the circumstances. Alcohol is not reimbursed as a standalone expense.

## 6. Home Office Equipment

Employees may request reimbursement for approved home-office equipment required for their role.

Equipment purchases exceeding **$500** require manager approval before purchase.

## 7. Software and Technology Purchases

Employees must not purchase software subscriptions, cloud services, or other technology on behalf of the company without following the company's procurement process.

This includes AI services and generative-AI subscriptions.

## 8. Personal Expenses

The company does not reimburse:

- Personal entertainment.
- Commuting between an employee's home and regular work location.
- Fines or penalties.
- Personal travel expenses.
- Expenses for family members unless explicitly approved as part of a company event.

## 9. Questions

Questions regarding reimbursement should be directed to Finance before making significant or unusual purchases.
