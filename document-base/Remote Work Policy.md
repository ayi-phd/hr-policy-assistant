# Remote Work Policy

**Policy Owner:** People Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All U.S.-based employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for remote and hybrid work arrangements while ensuring that employees can effectively collaborate, maintain appropriate security controls, and protect company and patient information.

## 2. Hybrid Work

Employees designated as hybrid are generally expected to work from their assigned company office at least two days per week. Individual teams may establish additional in-office requirements based on business needs.

Employees must maintain a primary work location in the United States that has been approved by their manager.

## 3. Working From Another U.S. State

Employees may temporarily work from another U.S. state for up to **10 business days per calendar year** without changing their primary work location.

The employee must:

- Obtain manager approval before beginning the temporary arrangement.
- Continue to comply with all company security requirements.
- Maintain a secure and private working environment.
- Use company-managed devices and approved network connections.

Requests exceeding 10 business days require approval from People Operations and may require additional review by Finance or Legal because of payroll, tax, employment, or regulatory considerations.

## 4. Working From Another Country

Employees may not perform work from outside the United States without prior written approval from People Operations and Legal.

International work may create tax, employment, data-protection, export-control, or regulatory obligations.

## 5. Patient and Confidential Information

Employees working remotely must take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access to company confidential information, protected health information (PHI), personal information, and customer data.

Employees must not access confidential information from public computers or unsecured public networks.

## 6. Remote Work Expenses

The company provides standard equipment required for an employee's role.

Reasonable business expenses associated with approved remote work must follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 7. Exceptions

Managers may approve temporary exceptions to hybrid-work requirements for documented business or personal circumstances. Permanent changes to an employee's primary work location require People Operations approval.
