# Parental Leave Policy

**Policy Owner:** People Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** Eligible U.S. employees

## 1. Purpose

The company provides paid parental leave to eligible employees following the birth, adoption, or placement of a child.

## 2. Eligibility

Regular full-time employees who have completed at least **six months of continuous employment** are eligible for company-paid parental leave.

Eligibility for statutory or protected leave may differ and is governed by applicable law.

## 3. Paid Parental Leave

Eligible employees may receive up to **12 weeks of paid parental leave**.

Parental leave must generally be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or placement of a child.

Leave may be taken continuously or, with People Operations approval, in separate blocks of at least two weeks.

## 4. Coordination With Other Leave

Company-paid parental leave may run concurrently with applicable federal, state, or local protected leave when permitted or required by law.

Employees should contact People Operations to determine how company leave interacts with statutory benefits.

## 5. Massachusetts Employees

Employees working in Massachusetts may qualify for additional benefits or protections under Massachusetts leave programs.

Company-paid parental leave does not eliminate or reduce statutory rights where applicable law provides greater benefits.

## 6. Notice

Employees should provide People Operations with at least **30 days' notice** of planned parental leave when the need for leave is foreseeable.

When 30 days' notice is not practical, employees should provide notice as soon as reasonably possible.

## 7. Benefits During Leave

Eligible employees generally continue to participate in company benefit programs during approved paid parental leave under the same employee contribution requirements that normally apply.

## 8. Return to Work

Employees should coordinate their expected return date with People Operations.

Employees returning from protected leave will receive the rights and protections required by applicable law.
