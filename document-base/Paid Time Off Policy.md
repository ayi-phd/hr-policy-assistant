# Paid Time Off Policy

**Policy Owner:** People Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** Full-time U.S. employees

## 1. Purpose

The company provides paid time off (PTO) to allow employees to take vacation, attend to personal matters, and maintain a healthy work-life balance.

## 2. PTO Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO based on their length of service.

| Years of Service | Annual PTO |
|---|---:|
| Less than 2 years | 15 days |
| 2–5 years | 20 days |
| More than 5 years | 25 days |

PTO is accrued each pay period.

## 3. Requesting PTO

Employees should submit planned PTO requests through the company's HR system at least **10 business days in advance** whenever practical.

Managers are responsible for ensuring adequate team coverage and may request alternative dates when multiple team members request overlapping time off.

Employees should not make non-refundable travel arrangements until PTO has been approved.

## 4. Sick Time

Employees may use accrued PTO for illness or medical appointments when permitted by applicable law and company policy.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably practical when they cannot work due to illness.

## 5. Carryover

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** into the following calendar year.

Unused days above the carryover limit expire at the end of the year unless otherwise required by applicable law.

## 6. Massachusetts Employees

Employees working in Massachusetts may be subject to additional state or local paid-leave requirements. Where applicable law provides greater rights than this policy, the company will comply with applicable law.

## 7. Extended Leave

PTO does not replace formal medical, parental, disability, or other protected leave programs. Employees requiring extended leave should contact People Operations.

## 8. Questions

Questions regarding PTO balances or eligibility should be directed to People Operations.
