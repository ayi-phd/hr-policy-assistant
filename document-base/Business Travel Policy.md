# Business Travel Policy

**Policy Owner:** Finance and People Operations  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for business travel and reimbursement of reasonable business-related travel expenses.

## 2. Travel Approval

Domestic travel requires manager approval before booking.

International travel requires manager approval and may require additional review by Legal or Security depending on the destination and purpose of travel.

Employees should use the company's designated travel booking service whenever practical.

## 3. Air Travel

Employees should book economy-class airfare for flights under six hours.

Premium economy or business class may be approved for flights exceeding six hours when justified by business needs.

First-class travel is not reimbursable unless explicitly approved by an executive officer.

## 4. Hotels

Employees should select reasonably priced business hotels near the work location.

Luxury accommodations are not reimbursable unless required because of a company-sponsored event or lack of reasonable alternatives.

## 5. Meals

Employees may expense reasonable meals incurred during approved business travel.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

When meals are provided by the company, a conference, or a business partner, employees should not submit an additional meal expense for the same meal.

## 6. Transportation

Reasonable expenses for rental cars, public transportation, taxis, and rideshare services are reimbursable when required for business purposes.

Employees should use public transportation when practical in locations where it provides a reasonable alternative.

## 7. Expense Submission

Travel expenses must be submitted through the expense management system within **15 calendar days** after returning from the trip.

Receipts are required for individual expenses of **$50 or more**.

## 8. International Travel

Employees traveling internationally on company business must comply with company security requirements and may be required to use company-managed devices.

Employees must not transport or access regulated, confidential, or patient information internationally unless specifically authorized.

## 9. Personal Travel

Personal travel combined with a business trip must be approved by the employee's manager.

The company will reimburse only expenses that would have been incurred for the business portion of the trip.
